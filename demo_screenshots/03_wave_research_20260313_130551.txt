*   **Authenticity & Verification:** This generation is skeptical of "greenwashing." They actively check labels (21%) and look for specific certifications. They prefer brands that act as partners in their sustainability journey rather than just selling products.

### 2. Hibiscus and Exotic Floral Scent Trends (2024-2026)
*   **The "Hibiscus Boom":** The hibiscus market is projected to grow significantly (CAGR of 7.1%), driven by its integration into "natural" cosmetics and functional wellness products. *   **Fragrance Wardrobes:** Gen Z is moving away from "signature scents" toward "fragrance wardrobes" that reflect different moods, daily emotions, and "sensorial rituals."

*   **Tactile Comfort:** There is a link between Gen Z’s rising anxiety and their preference for soft, natural fibers (like cotton) and products that provide "sensory regulation" through tactile softness.

*   **Green Chemistry:** Innovations in "bio-based surfactants" and "enzyme-powered formulas" are allowing brands to meet performance standards (low yellowing, superior softening) while using biodegradable, plant-based ingredients.
