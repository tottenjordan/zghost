*   **Traditional Brand Response:** Established brands (like Tide/P&G, Unilever, and SC Johnson) are increasingly launching "dilute-at-home" models and fabric enhancers in eco-friendly formats to compete with disruptive, digitally-native refill brands like Blueland or Grove Collaborative.
