### Top 3 Areas for Improvement
1.  **Trust Substantiation:** While the USDA Biobased seal is mentioned, it needs to be a primary visual focus to overcome Gen Z's "greenwashing" skepticism.
2.  **Narrative Flow:** Ensure the transition between the abstract macro fiber world and the urban "druid" setting is seamless to avoid losing the viewer.
3.  **Functional Clarity:** Explicitly highlight the "Cold-Water Optimization" to reassure eco-conscious users that the product won't leave residue.
