### 1. Rise of #CleanTok and Sustainable Living (2024–2025)
*   **Viral Influence:** #CleanTok has become a dominant cultural force with nearly **150 billion views**, outperforming even #BeautyTok. More than half of TikTok users report purchasing a household product after seeing it on the platform.
*   **The Gen Z Factor:** This generation is redefining housekeeping as "fun" and "rewarding," often seeking out "oddly satisfying" cleaning content. They prioritize brands that offer transparency, ethical practices, and aesthetic design.
*   **Market Growth:** The global sustainable cleaning market is projected to reach **$110 billion by 2025**, with eco-friendly products growing at twice the rate of traditional chemical cleaners. By 2026, sustainable cleaning is expected to account for over **50.7% of the total global market**.

### 2. Consumer Motivations: DIY vs. Traditional Cleaners
Consumers are increasingly balancing the trade-offs between homemade solutions and store-bought chemical cleaners:
*   **Health & Safety:** A primary driver is the "Low Tox Life" movement. Awareness of **Volatile Organic Compounds (VOCs)** in traditional cleaners has led consumers to seek plant-based alternatives to avoid respiratory issues, skin irritation, and risks to pets and children.
*   **Cost-Effectiveness:** DIY solutions (using vinegar, baking soda, and citric acid) are favored for being "pennies per bottle." However, consumers acknowledge they can be more time-consuming and sometimes less effective on heavy grease compared to professional-grade chemical cleaners.
*   **Sustainability:** Reducing single-use plastic is a major motivator. This has fueled the rise of **concentrated refills** and powder-to-liquid formats (e.g., Blueland, Grove Collaborative) that allow users to reuse glass or heavy-duty plastic bottles.

### 3. Cultural Shift and Influencer Impact
*   **"Deinfluencing" and Overconsumption:** While viral trends boost sales, a counter-movement against "product overload" is rising. Creators are now focusing on "minimalist cleaning" and using fewer, high-quality, multi-purpose products.
*   **Cleaning as Mental Health:** There is a growing trend of "in-the-flow" cleaning—shorter, more frequent bursts of tidying that provide an emotional boost and a sense of control in a volatile world. For many, a clean home is now synonymous with a "sanctuary."
*   **Brand Adaptation:** Major players like Unilever (via its Cleanipedia brand) are actively engaging with #CleanTok to provide "hacks" and educational content, successfully converting social media followers into customers by adopting the language of influencers.
