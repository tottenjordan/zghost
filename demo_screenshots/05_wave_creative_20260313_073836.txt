### 3. Concept Name: Cold-Water Botanical Bloom
*   **Type:** Video (Veo)
*   **Trend(s):** Cold-Water Scent Release, Botanical Realism, Eco-Anxiety (Energy Efficiency).
*   **Creative Concept:** An abstract, artistic representation of scent technology. It visualizes the "bloom" of fragrance molecules in cold water using a macro POV inside the machine.
*   **Detailed Rationale:** This addresses the "Cold-Water Challenge" identified in research. Consumers fear cold water "mutes" scent. By visualizing the fragrance as "blooming" hibiscus petals within a cold, refreshing environment, we provide a visual metaphor for the product's "Cold-Water Scent Release" technology. This appeals to the eco-conscious consumer looking for performance without compromise.
*   **Draft Prompt:** 
    > **SUPPRESS SUBTITLES. <SUBJECT>** Ethereal, translucent hibiscus petals made of vibrant pink light and liquid fragrance molecules. **<ACTION>** Inside the stainless steel drum of a washing machine, as crisp, clear cold water ripples and swirls around soft, white linen fabrics, the pink hibiscus "petals" begin to "bloom" and dissolve. They expand in slow motion, releasing swirls of vibrant cherry red and sunset amber hues into the cool blue-toned water. Tiny, refreshing oxygen bubbles cling to the fabric fibers. **<SCENE_AND_CONTEXT>** A macro POV shot from inside the washing machine drum, looking through the water. The metallic surface of the drum reflects the dancing pink lights. **<CAMERA_MOVEMENTS>** Handheld cam movement with a slight, rhythmic shake to mimic the gentle agitation of a high-efficiency washing cycle. **<LENS_AND_OPTICAL_EFFECTS>** Wide-angle macro lens capturing the distortion of the water and the grand scale of the "blooming" scent. **<VISUAL_STYLE_AND_AESTHETICS>** A high-contrast color palette of "Icy Blue" water and "Vibrant Hibiscus" pinks. Lighting features "volumetric lighting" rays piercing through the water, creating a magical, "Biophilic" atmosphere. 8k, photorealistic, artistic and atmospheric. **</VISUAL_STYLE_AND_AESTHETICS>**
