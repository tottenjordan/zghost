### 1. Trend-Driven Storyboard Plan

This commercial connects the **#ThriftTok** sustainable fashion movement with the **#SundayReset** wellness ritual, positioning Tide Hibiscus as the bridge between garment longevity and mental well-being.

**Character Sheet (Primary Subject):**
A 23-year-old woman of mixed heritage (East Asian and Caucasian) with a serene, peaceful expression. **Product Sheet (Visual Representation):**
A minimalist, heavy-bottomed clear glass laundry dispenser with a natural light-oak wooden cap. Inside is a vibrant, translucent pink botanical liquid that catches the light, showing elegant bubbles and viscous swirls. The dispenser has a matte white rectangular label with clean, modern black typography and a delicate gold-foil hibiscus flower illustration. It sits prominently on a polished white marble countertop or a rustic oak side table, surrounded by soft-focus natural elements like a fresh hibiscus flower or a green monstera leaf.

**Narrative Arc:**
*   **Scene 1 (Hook + Connection):** Set in a sun-drenched "thrifter's studio." Our character (Character Sheet) appreciates the texture of a vintage linen dress, highlighting the **#SlowFashion** trend. She reaches for the Tide Hibiscus dispenser (Product Sheet) sitting on a rustic table.
*   **Scene 2 (Demo + CTA):** Transitions to a bright, **#CleanTok** aesthetic laundry room. The commercial closes on her serene expression and the product, landing the message: *"Protect Your Style. Try Self-Care Laundry."*
