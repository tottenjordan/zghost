### Concept 1: The Olfactory Base
*   **Trend(s):** "Fragrance Wardrobes," "Clean Girl" Aesthetic, Editorial Product Photography.
*   **Creative Explanation:** This concept borrows from high-end perfume advertising. It positions Tide Hibiscus not as a "cleaning product" but as the first layer of a beauty routine. We focus on the *texture* of the fabric and the *vibrancy* of the flower.
*   **Draft Prompt (Gemini 3 Pro):**
    > A high-end editorial advertisement for Tide Hibiscus Fabric Softener. A close-up shot features a soft, premium cream-colored silk blouse draped elegantly over a warm white oak chair. In the soft-focus background, a vibrant pink Hibiscus flower and the Tide Eco-Box sit on a natural stone countertop. The lighting is "Golden Hour," streaming through a large window, highlighting the macro-textures of the silk fibers. Elegant, unlacquered brass laundry accessories are visible nearby. Cinematic, 8k resolution, minimalist luxury aesthetic, muted earthy tones with a pop of hibiscus pink.

### Concept 2: The Satisfying Pour (Video)
*   **Trend(s):** Restock Culture, Cleaning ASMR, Minimalist Organization.
*   **Creative Explanation:** An 8-second high-speed camera shot focusing on the sensory satisfaction of using the Eco-Box. It highlights the functional benefit of the "no-drip" tap while leaning into the visual appeal of the vibrant pink formula.
*   **Draft Prompt (VEO 3.1):**
    > **SUPPRESS SUBTITLES**
    > **<SUBJECT>** A close-up of a human hand with a clean, neutral-toned manicure. The subject is the black twist-tap of the Tide Eco-Box.
    > **<ACTION>** The fingers slowly rotate the tap. A thick, vibrant, translucent pink liquid flows smoothly and satisfyingly into a minimalist clear glass detergent bottle. The liquid has a shimmering, pearlized quality.
    > **<SCENE_AND_CONTEXT>** A bright, modern laundry room with warm wood cabinetry and a natural stone backsplash. The sun creates a soft lens flare.
    > **<CAMERA_ANGLE>** Extreme close-up on the tap and the liquid flow.
    > **<CAMERA_MOVEMENTS>** Slow-motion, static shot to emphasize the liquid's viscosity and the "ASMR" quality.
    > **<LENS_AND_OPTICAL_EFFECTS>** Macro lens, shallow depth of field (bokeh background), emphasizing the shimmer of the liquid.
    > **<VISUAL_STYLE_AND_AESTHETICS>** Natural morning light, photorealistic, 8K, peaceful and organized mood.

### Concept 3: The 14-Day Bloom (Video)
*   **Trend(s):** "Cleaning Therapy," Scent-Layering, Botanical Realism.
*   **Creative Explanation:** This concept visualizes the "microencapsulation technology" that makes the scent last. Instead of showing the bottle, we show the *experience* of the fragrance trail in a wellness-focused setting.
*   **Draft Prompt (VEO 3.1):**
    > **SUPPRESS SUBTITLES**
    > **<SUBJECT>** A Gen Z woman with a "Clean Girl" aesthetic, wearing a white linen button-down shirt.
    > **<ACTION>** She is walking through a sun-drenched outdoor garden. As she moves, ethereal, faint pink semi-transparent floral particles and soft gradients trail subtly behind her shoulders and sleeves.
    > **<SCENE_AND_CONTEXT>** A high-end botanical garden at midday. 
    > **<CAMERA_ANGLE>** Over-the-shoulder shot, tracking her movement from behind.
    > **<CAMERA_MOVEMENTS>** Slow, smooth tracking shot (Truck right).
    > **<LENS_AND_OPTICAL_EFFECTS>** Wide-angle lens, lens flare as she moves into the sunlight, shallow depth of field.
    > **<VISUAL_STYLE_AND_AESTHETICS>** Dreamy, whimsical, soft femininity. The scent trail is stylized as a "living fragrance" that looks natural and airy, not magical. Vibrant tropical colors.

### Concept 4: Botanical Efficacy
*   **Trend(s):** Low-Tox Living, Brand Transparency ("Receipts"), Sustainable Materiality.
*   **Creative Explanation:** This targets the skeptical consumer who wants proof of performance. It highlights the "fiber-breathability" mentioned in the research by showing the result on "wellness fabrics" alongside the sustainable Eco-Box.
*   **Draft Prompt (Gemini 3 Pro):**
    > A split-composition image for a "Sustainable Self-Care" campaign. On the left side: A macro, ultra-detailed shot of white cotton and linen fibers, looking exceptionally soft, fluffy, and breathable, with no waxy residue visible. On the right side: The Tide Eco-Box for Hibiscus Scent, showing the "60% less plastic" claim and FSC-certified logo clearly. The overall backdrop is a minimalist, airy room with soft shadows and botanical leaf patterns. Natural textures, photorealistic, clean and clinical but with a warm, human touch. Lighting is bright and even.
