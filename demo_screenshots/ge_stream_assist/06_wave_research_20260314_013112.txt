## Search Trend: Sustainable Laundry
There is a growing consumer interest in eco-friendly laundry products and sustainable cleaning solutions. This trend highlights a shift toward "green chemistry" and transparency in ingredient sourcing, as viewers seek functional ways to reduce plastic waste in their daily routines.

## YouTube Trend: Eco Cleaning Hacks
The trending "Eco Cleaning Hacks" content centers on the thesis that **transitioning to a sustainable cleaning routine is both accessible and cost-effective** for the average consumer.

*   **Main Thesis:** The movement argues that natural ingredients (like vinegar, baking soda, and essential oils) can match the efficacy of traditional cleaners while improving indoor air quality.
*   **CleanTok Influence:** Trending due to the "CleanTok" and "Sustainable Living" movements, creators demonstrate "low-waste" routines using plant-based concentrates and refillable glass containers.
*   **DIY Customization:** Content often features tutorials on mixing custom scents using hibiscus and citrus, emphasizing "progress over perfection" in the journey toward a zero-waste household.
