#### **Top 3 Areas for Improvement**
1.  **Packaging Education:** While "biodegradable" is mentioned, panelists wanted more visual proof or detail on the packaging innovation.
2.  **Authenticity Balance:** Some panelists felt the production was *too* perfect; a slightly more "lo-fi" version might resonate better on TikTok.
3.  **Emotional Depth:** The "escape" narrative is effective but could be deepened by showing more diverse "ritual" moments.
