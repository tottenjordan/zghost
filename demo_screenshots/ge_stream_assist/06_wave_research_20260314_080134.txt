*   **Aesthetic Alignment:** Gen Z chooses products based on "mood and aesthetic." A hibiscus-scented product fits into a "vibrant," "tropical," or "fresh" lifestyle aesthetic often shared on social media.

### 3. Sustainability: Ingredients & Packaging
*   **Plant-Based Shift:** 85% of consumers view plant-based products favorably. Gen Z specifically looks for "clean," "non-toxic," and "biodegradable" labels to avoid "greenwashing."
