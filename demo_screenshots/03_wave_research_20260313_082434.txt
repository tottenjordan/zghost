*   **Health as a New Luxury:** For many, the ability to maintain a toxin-free environment is viewed as a form of self-care. This has shifted the perception of laundry from a chore to a sensory wellness ritual.

*   **Fragrance Transparency:** Consumers are increasingly wary of the "Fragrance/Parfum" label loophole. There is a strong preference for **transparent ingredient lists** and fragrances derived from essential oils or plant-based isolates.

*   **Economic Viability:** Zero-waste strategies are no longer just for niche brands. Large CPG companies are adopting these formats to mitigate rising plastic costs and appeal to the 66% of global consumers who report a willingness to pay more for sustainable brands.
