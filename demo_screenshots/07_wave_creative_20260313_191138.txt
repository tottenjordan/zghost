They wear a high-performance, oversized technical utility vest in a matte slate-grey finish with silver hardware. The camera performs a slow dolly-in as giant, hyper-realistic hibiscus flowers in vibrant shades of magenta and coral begin to bloom rapidly from the cracks in the grey concrete around their feet. The visual style is tactile and slightly grainy, avoiding over-polished smoothness to feel like a high-end fashion film. Subtle atmospheric dust motes float in the air.
