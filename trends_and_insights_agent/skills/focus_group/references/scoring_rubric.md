# Focus Group Scoring Rubric

## Category Definitions and Weights

### 1. Visual Quality (20%)

Evaluates the overall visual fidelity and production value of the commercial.

| Score | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 9-10 | Broadcast-quality visuals. Professional lighting, color grading, and composition. No visible artifacts. |
| 7-8 | High quality with minor imperfections. Good composition and lighting. Minimal artifacts. |
| 5-6 | Acceptable quality. Some noticeable issues with lighting, color, or artifacts. |
| 3-4 | Below average. Multiple visual issues that distract from the content. |
| 1-2 | Poor quality. Significant artifacts, poor lighting, or unprofessional composition. |

### 2. Narrative Consistency (20%)

Assesses story coherence and character/visual consistency across all scenes.

| Score | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 9-10 | Seamless story flow. Characters maintain perfect consistency. Transitions feel natural. |
| 7-8 | Strong narrative with minor consistency gaps. Story is clear and engaging. |
| 5-6 | Story is understandable but has noticeable jumps or character inconsistencies. |
| 3-4 | Disjointed narrative. Significant character or visual inconsistencies between scenes. |
| 1-2 | Incoherent story. Major breaks in continuity that confuse the viewer. |

### 3. Trend Relevance (15%)

Measures how naturally and authentically the trending topic is integrated.

| Score | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 9-10 | Trend feels like the organic inspiration for the commercial. Cultural reference is immediately recognizable and authentic. |
| 7-8 | Trend is well-integrated and adds value. Target audience would recognize the reference. |
| 5-6 | Trend is present but feels somewhat forced or surface-level. |
| 3-4 | Trend connection is weak or could be confused with generic content. |
| 1-2 | Trend is absent or feels entirely disconnected from the commercial. |

### 4. Product Integration (15%)

Evaluates whether the product placement feels natural and the brand is visible.

| Score | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 9-10 | Product appears naturally as part of the story. Brand is clearly visible without being intrusive. |
| 7-8 | Good product integration. Brand presence is clear and mostly natural. |
| 5-6 | Product is visible but placement feels somewhat forced or awkward. |
| 3-4 | Product feels shoehorned in. Brand visibility is poor or overly aggressive. |
| 1-2 | Product is barely visible or completely disrupts the narrative flow. |

### 5. Emotional Impact (15%)

Measures the commercial's ability to evoke the intended emotional response.

| Score | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 9-10 | Powerful emotional resonance. Viewer feels compelled to act or share. Memorable. |
| 7-8 | Strong emotional connection. Commercial is engaging and leaves an impression. |
| 5-6 | Some emotional engagement but doesn't leave a lasting impression. |
| 3-4 | Flat emotional response. Commercial feels generic or forgettable. |
| 1-2 | No emotional connection. Viewer is indifferent or negatively impacted. |

### 6. Audience Appeal (15%)

Assesses likelihood of engagement from the target demographic.

| Score | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 9-10 | Target audience would enthusiastically share and discuss this. High viral potential. |
| 7-8 | Strong appeal to target audience. Would generate positive engagement. |
| 5-6 | Moderate appeal. Target audience might watch but unlikely to share. |
| 3-4 | Weak appeal. Content doesn't resonate with target demographic. |
| 1-2 | No appeal or potentially alienating to target audience. |

## GO/NO-GO Thresholds

- **GO**: Overall weighted score >= 6.0
- **NO-GO**: Overall weighted score < 6.0

## Predicted Uplift Categories

| Level | Score Range | Description |
|-------|------------|-------------|
| Very High | 8.0-10.0 | Exceptional commercial likely to outperform benchmarks significantly |
| High | 7.0-7.9 | Strong commercial expected to perform above average |
| Medium | 5.5-6.9 | Solid commercial expected to perform at or slightly above average |
| Low | Below 5.5 | Commercial may underperform without significant revisions |
